Frequently asked questions

What is Discourse?

Discourse is a native client for Matrix, an open, decentralized, end-to-end encrypted messaging network. It is not tied to any single provider — you sign in to whichever Matrix homeserver you use.

How do I sign in?

Open the app and enter your Matrix account (for example @you:matrix.org) or your homeserver address, then authenticate. Discourse supports both password and single-sign-on login depending on what your homeserver offers.

I'm not receiving notifications.

Check that notifications are allowed for Discourse in iOS Settings → Notifications → Discourse, and that notifications are enabled for the account inside the app's Settings tab. Notifications are delivered through your homeserver's push gateway, so a homeserver that isn't configured for push won't send them.

Can I use more than one account?

Yes. You can be signed in to multiple Matrix accounts at once and switch between them. You'll receive notifications for every signed-in account, and each account has its own notification toggle in Settings.

Are my messages private?

Messages in encrypted rooms are end-to-end encrypted on your device — not even your homeserver can read them. Discourse itself has no servers and collects no data about you. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Sign out of an account or delete the app to remove its locally stored data from your device. To deactivate or delete your Matrix account itself, use the tools provided by your homeserver.
