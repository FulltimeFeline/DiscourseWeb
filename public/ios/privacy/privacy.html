Discourse for iOSA Matrix chat client for iPhone & iPad

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Discourse for iOS is a chat client for the Matrix open communication network. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app on your iPhone or iPad. The short version: Discourse has no servers of its own and collects nothing about you.

We don't run a backend. Discourse connects directly to whichever Matrix homeserver you sign in to. Your messages, contacts, and account live on that homeserver — operated by you or by whoever runs it — not with us. We never see your traffic.

What the app stores

Everything Discourse keeps is stored locally on your device:

  • Your login session and end-to-end encryption keys, held in the iOS keychain.
  • A local cache of your rooms, messages, and media so the app works quickly and offline.
  • Your in-app preferences and settings.

This data never leaves your device except to communicate with the homeserver you chose. Signing out of an account or deleting the app clears its local data.

What we collect

Nothing. Discourse contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that phone home. We do not have accounts, servers, or logs, so there is no personal information for us to collect, sell, or share.

Your homeserver

Because Discourse talks directly to your Matrix homeserver, the operator of that homeserver handles your account data and is governed by their privacy policy. If you use a public homeserver such as matrix.org, review that provider's terms. In end-to-end encrypted rooms, message content is encrypted on your device and the homeserver cannot read it.

Push notifications

If you enable notifications, your homeserver sends a push through a Matrix push gateway to Apple's Push Notification service, which delivers it to your device. To keep message contents private, pushes are configured to carry only the identifiers needed to fetch and decrypt the message on your device; the notification text is assembled locally by the app. Apple's handling of push tokens is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Media

Images, files, and other media you view or send are transferred directly between your device and your homeserver. Media you open is cached locally to avoid re-downloading.

Children

Discourse is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
